# Utility-Civil Bidding Defaults

**Control:** IHOS estimating standard  
**Applies to:** TELUS, BC Hydro, and comparable shallow-utility civil bids  
**Owner:** Iron House management  
**Approval gate:** Jeremie Peters or Mack Warren before external issue  
**Source:** Management direction recorded in issue #386 after review of the 31543 Lombard Avenue V2 estimate using Mac's TELUS metrics

## Purpose

Use this standard to keep utility-civil estimates consistent, auditable, and ready for project handoff. It defines default scope treatment, not permanent quantities or vendor prices.

A project-specific tender, drawing, specification, addendum, municipal standard, vendor quote, site condition, or explicit management instruction overrides these defaults. Every override must remain visible in the estimate with its reason and approver.

## Default included scope

Unless the project documents state otherwise, carry the following:

1. Utility scan and hydrovac/non-mechanical daylighting as required.
2. Trench cutting and excavation.
3. Pole hold or temporary stabilization allowance where work is near a pole or the drawing flags stability.
4. Haul all excavated spoil off site, with loads and disposal assumptions shown.
5. Full utility installation to the property line or drawing limit.
6. Imported 19 mm (3/4 in.) road-base backfill unless the governing specification requires another material.
7. Backfill placement in lifts and compaction.
8. Compaction testing.
9. Asphalt cutting, patching, and restoration allowance.
10. Topsoil and seed restoration.
11. Traffic and pedestrian control where affected.
12. Required inspection coordination and as-built/record information.

## Default supplied by others

Unless explicitly included and priced:

- Permits and authority fees are supplied and paid by the customer.
- TELUS, BC Hydro, or the customer supplies utility duct, fittings, service boxes, pilasters, and other drawing-specific utility materials.
- Iron House may supply these items only as a separately approved extra.

## Default exclusions and qualifications

- Arborist services are excluded unless specifically shown and priced.
- Rocks larger than 1 m3 are removed only on an approved time-and-material basis.
- Engineered pole support beyond the stated pole-hold allowance is an extra.
- Unknown utility relocation or support, energized electrical work, and utility cable work are by others unless explicitly priced.
- Blasting, contaminated soil, dewatering, unsuitable material replacement, premium/night work, and restoration beyond stated limits are excluded or separately allowed.
- Restoration quantities, inspection requirements, traffic controls, and vendor allowances remain visible and adjustable.
- Mechanical excavation near known plant follows the utility-owner and applicable safety requirements; required hand exposure or approved non-mechanical methods must be priced.

## Required estimating fields

Each utility-civil estimate must show:

- Drawing and specification basis.
- Quantity, unit, unit rate, and direct cost.
- Source or assumption for each line.
- Rate status: internal, allowance, vendor quote required, by others, excluded, or provisional.
- Labour, equipment, trucking, disposal, materials, subcontractors, testing, restoration, and indirect costs separately.
- Overhead, contingency, profit, GST, and rounding separately.
- Included scope, exclusions, qualifications, RFIs, and alternates.
- Estimate maturity and approval status.
- Change summary for any material revision.

## Override rule

A default may be changed only when the estimate records:

1. The default being changed.
2. The project-specific replacement.
3. The reason and supporting source.
4. The cost and schedule effect.
5. The person approving the override.

## Commercial controls

- Current Iron House rates or project-specific quotes govern pricing; this standard does not freeze vendor or internal rates.
- Vendor quotes are required for major traffic control, pole support, hydrovac, testing, asphalt, concrete, and other material subcontract scopes where the value or risk is material.
- No estimate becomes customer-ready without management review.
- No permanent job number is generated until written award or acceptance.
- Completed estimates must remain linked to their source drawings, RFIs, quote files, and project/opportunity record.

## IHOS implementation note

IHOS should expose this standard as a selectable default profile named **Utility Civil - TELUS / BC Hydro**. Applying the profile should populate the scope checklist, default qualifications, and required review prompts without silently populating project quantities or current dollar rates.
