# Equipment procedure QR field access

Issue #70 equipment QR access uses authenticated IHOS equipment field URLs. A QR contains only the equipment record URL; it does not contain a password, access token, rate, equipment details or safety content.

Administrators and operations managers intentionally assign references from the existing controlled procedure index. IHOS does not infer procedure applicability from an equipment name or type. Draft-review procedures cannot be assigned.

After sign-in, the field page shows the equipment identity and management-assigned references. If no reference is assigned, the page tells the user to contact the responsible supervisor before work. Procedure links open the current controlled Iron House safety program; printed or downloaded copies may be uncontrolled.

These references support access only. The responsible supervisor still verifies the equipment, task, operator competency, hazards, permits, current conditions and required controls.
