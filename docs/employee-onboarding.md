# Employee onboarding

IHOS employee onboarding is available to administrators under the employee onboarding module and to invited employees through a secure token link.

## Controlled positions

Field staff: Green Labourer, Labourer, Skilled Labourer, Junior Pipelayer, Senior Pipelayer, Grademan / Top Man, Foreman, Superintendent.

Office staff: Admin, Controller, Project Manager, COO, CEO.

## Workflow

1. An administrator creates a draft onboarding record.
2. IHOS issues a hashed, expiring invitation token.
3. The employee opens the portal, saves progress, acknowledges the package, and submits it.
4. An administrator can request corrections or approve the submission.
5. Only approved onboarding can be activated into an employee record.

Production email delivery and activation remain subject to management approval and environment configuration.
